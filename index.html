Introduction

Welcome to Athkar_App — a clean, distraction-free space for Islamic remembrances (Athkar). We believe a spiritual app should respect your privacy as much as it respects your time.

This Privacy Policy describes how we handle information when you use the application. The short version: we don’t collect any.

Information We Collect

Athkar_App does not collect, process, transmit, or store any personally identifiable information — ever. The following are never requested or accessed:

Name Email address Phone number Location Contacts Photos & videos Messages Payment info Device identifiers Advertising IDs Browsing history Health data Biometrics

Account Registration

No account. No sign-in. No registration. Athkar_App is ready to use the moment you open it — no personal information required, ever.

Permissions

Athkar_App does not request access to any sensitive device permissions. The following are never asked for:

Camera Microphone Location Contacts Phone SMS Storage Calendar
The app works completely offline and requires no access to personal device data to function.

Data Storage

Athkar_App has no backend servers and maintains no user database. Because no personal information is collected, there is nothing to store — on our end or anyone else’s.

Internet Connectivity

Athkar_App is fully offline-capable. No personal information is sent over the internet at any point during normal operation.

Third-Party Services

We do not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. The following SDKs and platforms are not used:

Firebase Google Analytics Google AdMob Firebase Crashlytics Facebook SDK Any ad/tracking SDK

Children’s Privacy

Athkar_App is designed for users of all ages. Because no personal information is ever collected, the app is inherently safe for children under 13 and compliant with applicable children’s privacy laws.

Data Security

The most secure data is data that is never collected. Since Athkar_App maintains no personal user database, there is nothing to breach, leak, or compromise.

We remain committed to secure development practices and regular app integrity reviews to protect the experience itself.

Your Rights

Since Athkar_App does not collect or retain any personal information, there is no data for you to access, correct, export, or delete. Your privacy is intact by design — not by policy alone.

Policy Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect app improvements or legal requirements. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised effective date. We encourage you to review it periodically.
